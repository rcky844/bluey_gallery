# A galley for Bluey
_Last edited: November 23, 2026_

This serves as a chronology to properly document the whale used by me, **Ricky Cheung**, in profile pictures since 2023.

It provides me with the option to share about my whale and backstory to people I meet, instead of having to repeat each time. And, it provides a place to shout my thoughts into for a few weeks.

The other purpose is that I hope this inspires more people to speak out and share their life experiences. If our DNA wants us to leave a trace on the planet, perhaps writing things publically serves that purpose quite well.

As a first of this kind to document my life, I decide it is appropriate still to make this available publically. After all, who wouldn't like a quite little whale roaming the oceans?
